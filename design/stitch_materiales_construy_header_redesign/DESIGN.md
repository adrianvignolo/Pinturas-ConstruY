## Brand & Style

This design system elevates industrial coatings, high-durability rubber paints, and architectural enamels into a refined, high-trust digital storefront. It merges the rugged dependability of heavy-duty materials with the bespoke visual clarity of an architectural studio. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Modern Technical Architecturalism**:
- Deep industrial navy grounds the interface with authoritative stability.
- High-energy terracotta/safety orange accents drive high-priority actions, quotes, and primary checkout funnels.
- Radiant amber and gold accents honor contractor loyalty tiers, point accruals, and technical certifications.
- Structured geometric typography instills clarity and precision, while subtle mechanical rounding softens industrial hardness into an ergonomic, modern e-commerce experience.

Targeting both professional contractors needing bulk technical specifications and retail homeowners seeking premium interior finishes, the experience balances dense material specs with elegant, conversion-focused retail patterns.

## Colors

The palette directly anchors the corporate authority of the manufacturing facility while providing crisp accents that drive immediate visual hierarchy.

- **Primary (`#0B2545`)**: The core brand anchor. Represents structural integrity, deep pigment bases, and corporate security. Used for primary navigation, main headings, dark-mode callout blocks, and persistent frame elements.
- **Secondary (`#E84B18`)**: An energized industrial cadmium-orange. Directly borrowed from safety markings and industrial machinery, this color is reserved for primary CTAs, promotional badges, cart additions, and price emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#F7B731`)**: Warm architectural amber/gold. Applied systematically across partner rewards, loyalty points tickers, star ratings, and membership tiers (Auyantepuy / Roraima).
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate gray providing balanced mid-tone contrast for meta information, item codes, specifications, and secondary borders.
- **Canvas & Structure (`#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp clean off-white background ensuring paint swatches and can renderings retain 100% color fidelity without warm or cool tint contamination.
- **WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`)**: Standardized utility tone for immediate quote generation and direct-to-workshop support lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies entirely on **Poppins** for headlines, body, labels, and technical specifications, providing a unified, clean geometric sans-serif aesthetic.

- **Poppins** delivers a modern, highly legible geometric structure. All section headers, banner announcements, category indicators, body text, and pricing tables leverage Poppins' weights from regular to bold, ensuring exceptional readability across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Spec Codes**: Color chips and chemical catalog numbers (`spec-code`) utilize a tight, tracked variant to ensure formulation codes like `M9010-261A` or `FE-RAL5010` stand distinct from marketing prose.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a responsive 12-column layout on desktop, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, max container width `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` external canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Complex dual tables (such as B2B Wholesale vs. Retail tiers) collapse into segmented views.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Catalog grids reflow to 2 columns for paint canisters and swatches, while technical charts utilize horizontal scroll wrappers with edge fades.

Spacing rhythm is strictly built on an 8pt base grid (`space-sm: 0.5rem`, `space-md: 1rem`, `space-lg: 1.5rem`), reserving `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` exclusively for structural zoning between promotional hero banners, swatches, and partner programs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines clean, low-contrast ghost outlines with deep, architectural ambient drop shadows for interactive elements.

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base)**: `#F8FAFC` base page background with `#E2E8F0` hairline borders (`1px solid`). Applied to inactive color swatch chips and structural product grids.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift / Product Cards)**: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04)`. Outlined with `#E2E8F0`. Lifts product buckets off the surface while maintaining catalog clarity.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States)**: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04)`. Card translates `-2px` on the Y-axis. The border shifts from `#E2E8F0` to a subtle `#0B2545` (15% opacity).
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Drawers & Modals)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(7, 23, 43, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(7, 23, 43, 0.08)`. Applied to sticky reward tickers, wholesale calculator overlays, and mobile navigation sheets.
- **Industrial Contrast Blocks**: Solid `#0B2545` surface containers use negative elevation with white borders at 10% opacity, providing visual anchoring for B2B partner sections like "Auyantepuy" and "Roraima".

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 2`, the UI relies on an exact `0.5rem` (8px) base radius. This prevents the interface from feeling dangerously sharp or playfully soft, perfectly aligning with modern architectural products.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to standard buttons, input fields, search bars, color chips, and product container cards.
- **Large Radius (`1rem`)**: Reserved for primary hero visual cards, promotional banner wrappers, and partner tier feature cards.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem`)**: Applied to elevated bottom sheets on mobile devices and dialog overlays.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Strictly reserved for micro-badges (e.g., stock indicators, "Clase A", "Lavable"), reward point stars, and category filter pills (`Chips`).

- **Primary Action (Secondary Color)**: Background `#E84B18`, text white, `0.5rem` border-radius, font `Poppins SemiBold` (14px). Hover state transitions to `#F05A28` with a subtle elevation lift (`Level 2`).
- **Partner / B2B Action**: Background `#0B2545`, text white, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Hover state: fills with `#07172B`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0B2545`, text `#0B2545`. Hover state: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Floating WhatsApp**: Circular `3.5rem` or pill-shaped, `#25D366` background, white icon, ambient shadow `0 8px 16px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.3)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips**: Height `2.25rem`, padding `0 1rem`, pill-rounded (`9999px`). Default state: white background, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#64748B`. Active state: background `#0B2545`, text white, border `#0B2545`.
- **Color Formulation Swatch**: Square `4rem x 4rem` with `0.5rem` rounding. Upper 70% shows real paint tint; bottom 30% features an integrated `#FFFFFF` caption strip showing color code and name in `spec-code` typography.

### Loyalty & Rewards Ticker
- **Persistent Header Ribbon**: Background `#0B2545`, border-bottom `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Font `spec-code` in `#F8FAFC`. Points values highlighted with tertiary `#F7B731` paired with a 12px gold star icon. Hovering an item reveals the bucket volume redemption threshold.

### Cards & Presentation Grids
- **Product Presentation Card**: White background, `0.5rem` radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Product image centered over neutral `#F1F5F9` backdrop. Price formatted prominently in `headline-sm` with Venezuelan Bolívar (VES) value paired with muted USD reference (`body-sm`). Features a quick-add floating circular button with a `+` micro-icon.
- **Partner Tier Card (Auyantepuy / Roraima)**: Navy `#0B2545` or rich deep blue `#07172B` surface. Border `1px solid rgba(247, 183, 49, 0.3)` with custom gold badge header. Bullet list items use miniature `#E84B18` square indicators.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Catalog Search Field**: Height `3rem`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Search icon prefix in `#64748B`. Integrated CTA button nested inside the right edge (`#E84B18`). Focused state triggers a `2px` focus ring in `#0B2545` with zero blur.
- **Form Selectors**: Native-like clean styling with chevron indicators, active states marked by deep navy border transitions.
